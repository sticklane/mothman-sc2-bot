Empirical Audit of Mothman's Dual Mirror Brains (Loop 34 Replays + Honest Arena 2.0) #

Key Takeaway

Executive Bottom Line: Both Mirror Brains contain genuine high-value primitives buried inside large volumes of unconsumed telemetry, mismatched symbol contracts, and severe architectural bugs.

  • Strategic Mirror-Mind (lib/mirror-mind-predictor.rkt, 6,959 lines) suffers a strictly monotonic anti-depth curve: pure observational d=0 beats d=1, d=2, and d=3..5 in both realistic Fog-of-War (35.8% vs 31.8% vs 22.8% vs 10.5% dominant next-60s unit accuracy) and Perfect-Vision Oracle (45.6% vs 33.6% vs 18.0% vs 11.4%). In counterfactual A/B tests across 2,377 real replay checkpoints, stripping pure Mirror-Mind predictions changes step-priority-macro-brain scheduled-ids on only 3.9% of checkpoints (saving-for 6.6%, desired-bases 16.7%) — and 62.5% (25/40) of audited diffs were actively harmful (chiefly a bug where defend-All-In-Then-Counter-Expand recommends our_bases + 1 100% of the time, forcing 1:40 3rd-Hatchery escrows against Terran rushes!). Meanwhile, mirror-strategy is 100% dead across the entire bot due to disjoint symbol sets, mirror-enemy-upgrade-advantage never subtracts our own upgrades (25.4% wrong sign), and predict-multi-horizon-snapshots burns 29% of Mirror-Mind CPU purely for unread telemetry.
  • Tactical Mirror Brain (step-tactical-mirror-brain! in lib/tactics/arena-minimax.rkt, 4,167 lines) is fast (0.21 ms/step mean, 0.35 ms p95) and powers Zerg's 84W-0L Honest Arena dominance (76.1% of Zerg clash units survive to wire), yet lib/bot-runtime.rkt:28132–28862 silently bypasses 95.0% of Terran and 64.5% of Protoss tbrain outputs (88.6% T / 66.7% P in Arena!) — throwing away tbrain actions for Marines, Marauders, Stalkers, Adepts, Tanks, Vikings, Liberators, Thors, Ghosts, Reapers, Void Rays, Oracles, Carriers, Banelings, Ravagers, and Lurkers! Worse, compress-two-arena-sim-lists (arena-minimax.rkt:1690–1730) truncates armies to the first 3 units in list order (take 3), deleting 51.6% of distinct unit types in Arena (17.0% in replays) and causing 39.8% of real clashes and 58.3% of Arena scenarios to FLIP their chosen tactic solely by reversing the unit list order!

Methodology & Ground-Truth Calibration #

  • Replay Panel (30 Loop 34 .SC2Replays, zero repo edits, zero SC2 runs): 16 core_14 + 10 unseen_12 + 4 baseline_h2h (11 Wins, 17 Losses, 2 Ties; 11 Zerg, 10 Terran, 9 Protoss), covering rush (12PoolBot, Chance, Thessaloniki, zig-reapers), cloak (who DTs/Banshees, onlyfans Banshees, CryptBotRevival DTs), air (mini-puck Phoenixes, sc2ai_protoss Tempests, Creepy_strategies), siege/mech (RustyNikolaj, AxeFighter, Mulebot), and macro mirrors (puck, oberon, PolyMorph, Mothman-H2H).
  • Observation Fidelity (2,467 checkpoints at 10s resolution, 2,377 post-30s eval steps, 370 beacon-free combat clashes): Reconstructed from raw s2protocol tracker events (SUnitBorn, SUnitInit, SUnitDone, SUnitDied, SUnitTypeChange, SUnitPositions, SUpgrade, SPlayerStats) under two visibility modes:
    1. Realistic Fog-of-War (FOW): Enemy units visible only within SC2 sight radii (9.0–11.0t) of alive Mothman units/buildings, plus scouted-and-alive enemy structure snapshots.
    2. Perfect-Vision (ORACLE): All alive enemy entities visible (isolating Mirror-Mind model errors from scouting/FOW gaps).
  • Live Calibration Check: Across 48,182 real [SCBOT_STATE] lines from 252 live Loop 34 bot logs (iter34_wave34_full), live overall-accuracy (conf) was mean = 0.681, median = 0.670, min = 0.150, max = 0.980. Our FOW replay harness produced mean = 0.680, median = 0.670, min = 0.150, max = 0.980 — matching live SC2 behavior to 0.001.
