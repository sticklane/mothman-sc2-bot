Scouting & Hidden-Tech Clues

Note

Historical design note recorded after our first live ladder runs, detailing how scouting sightings trigger detection and defense.

Key Takeaway

Across 317 external AI Arena games through #5132989 (36 games on commit 49e1457a), our macro rewrite improved base count (2.38 -> 3.64), early workers (W@4m: 19.5 -> 25.2), peak workers (33.7 -> 48.4), and non-loss rate (12.5% -> 30.6%), but Combat Trade Efficiency collapsed (2.32x -> 0.56x, and 0.01x–0.20x in losses). Our bots are losing (or tying at 45:00 on 11 bases) because single-frame/lack-of-observation tricks blind our army sizing and engagement decisions, and three specific escrow/tech-gating bugs still deviate from our macro design.

2. Why enemy-visible Fails: Audit of the 6 Opponent-Knowledge Dimensions

Your intuition is exact: enemy-visible (and enemy-threat-supply) collapses a 6-dimensional belief problem into "are enemy combat units near our bases right now or in the last 10 seconds?" Both lack of observation and single transient observations actively deceive our planner:

DimensionHow Current Code Handles ItHow Single / Missing Observations Trick Us in Live Games
1. Enemy Army (Total Value & Composition)bot-runtime.rkt builds enemy-u-counts purely from this frame's visible enemy-units. bot-runtime.rkt forgets remembered-enemy-threat-supply after 224 frames (10.0s) and subtracts 0.85 * live-defending-force-supply.Lack of observation = "Enemy has 0 army": In #5131381 (Z vs SicToss), we sent 13 Changelings that saw 30 Stalkers + 6 Immortals (9,925 army value), yet because they stood > 55 tiles away (or stepped out of vision for 10s), enemy-threat-supply was 0, peaceful? was #t, and Zerg droned to 72 with only 1,400 army (7 Roaches, 12 Lings) before dying in one push (0.04x trade).
2. Enemy Army Position & Reachabilitymirror-mind-predictor.rkt computes reachability only over currently visible enemy-units, instead of propagating last-seen tag positions (x_0, y_0, t_0) outward as an expanding speed cone $r(t) = v_{\text{army}}(t - t_0)$.When an enemy 30-supply push leaves their natural and crosses mid-map (25–35s travel time), our 10s threat memory expires mid-transit, so we expand or drone right before impact, OR send small squads out on pos=attack directly into the fogged main army.
3. Enemy Bases (Confirmed vs Confirmed-Empty vs Unscouted)bot-runtime.rkt never passes #:enemy-building-counts to make-macro-world-state (it stays #f in live games!). 4. 6. Ignoring confirmed production capacity lets us sit on 2–3 Gateways/Barracks (1,400–1,700 army) against 6–8 facility pushes (8,000–10,000 army).

3. Other Concrete Deviations From Our Design Found in Recent Losses

Inspecting [SCBOT_PRIORITY] and [SCBOT_STATE] frame-by-frame in the match_log.zip archives exposed four additional implementation bugs where live play deviated directly from our intended design:

  1. Premature T3 Tech Escrows Blocking Core Army at 3:45 (#5132983, #5132984, #5131612):
    • Because Phase C added full 4+ base T3 tech nodes (ghost-academy, templar-archive, fleet-beacon) to the matchup specs, priority-macro-brain.rkt promoted them via macro-tech-plan (u = 478–494) before checking if our midgame army/production foundation existed!
    • Smoking gun (#5132983, f=5040 / 3:45 at 31 supply): saving_for=ghost-academy(floor=150M/100G) escrow_queue=(ghost-academy@478.1, marauder(blocked-by-escrow@425.0), marine(blocked-by-escrow@241.0)) — Terran froze Marine & Marauder production on 2 Barracks at 3:45 to save for a Ghost Academy! In #5132984 and #5131612, Protoss similarly locked 300M/200G on templar-archive + fleet-beacon while under-producing Gateway/Robo units.
  2. Full 0 -> 400M Expansion Escrow Freezes Early TownHalls for 20–25s (#5131383, #5132950, #5132985):
    • In #5131383 (f=1200..1756) and #5132950 (f=1452..1900), nexus/command-center (u = 620.0) set floor = 400M starting at 85M, blocking probe@488.0 / scv@488.0 for 550 frames (25s = 2+ lost workers right at 1:00)!
    • Pros never stop Probes/SCVs at 85M to save 400M: a TownHall takes 12s per worker (50M), while 16 workers mine ~960M/min (~190M per 12s), so banking only begins inside the imminent window (minerals >= 260M) while continuing nonstop worker production below 260M!
  3. Unplaced Scheduled Structure Spins Without Releasing Production (#5132950, #5132985):
    • In #5132950 (f=2004..2352), sched=(barracks@171..191) repeated for 350 frames at 375M with sup=18/23 frozen (0 SCVs trained because 1 CC was morphing Orbital and CC #2 was still building, capping 1-base max-useful-workers). In #5132985 (f=5004), Terran floated 495M/336G at 23 supply (4 SCVs gained in 4 minutes!).
  4. 45:00 Stalemates on 11 Bases Because Maxed Zerg Spams T1 Lings Into Siege (#5131434, #5132986):
    • 49e1457a got Zerg to 10–11 Hatcheries and 78–79 Drones against CutterSlayer and oberon, proving the 4+ base economy works — but Zerg built 308–338 Zerglings + 16 Spine Crawlers + 0 Lurkers / 0 Brood Lords / 0 Ultralisks, trading at 0.06x–0.49x into Bunkers and Siege Tanks until the 2700s clock expired!

4. Prioritized Architectural Recommendations

flowchart LR
    OBS["Raw SC2 Wire Observation + Visibility Grid"] --> BELIEF["Persistent 6D OpponentBeliefState (Tag Memory + Pad Sightings + Resource Bounds)"]
    BELIEF --> REGIME["Coupled Macro Regime & Army-Parity Floor (W*, B*, G*, A_min*)"]

P0 — Build a Single Persistent OpponentBeliefState Across All 6 Dimensions (Replaces enemy-visible)

Create a dedicated lib/opponent-belief.rkt module maintained across the entire match and passed into both make-macro-world-state (#:enemy-belief + #:enemy-building-counts) and Tactical Layer 3/4:

  1. Persistent Unit & Structure Registry by Tag:
    • Never drop an enemy unit from believed-enemy-units when it enters fog! Keep (tag, type, last-x, last-y, last-seen-frame, hp) until either (a) it dies in vision (raw_data.event.dead_units!), (b) our vision covers (last-x, last-y) within its movement cone and confirms absence, or (c) gradual confidence decay over 90s (2,016 frames, not 10s!).
    • Separate persistent army value from free temporary summons (locust, locust-flying, broodling, auto-turret, hallucination, changeling) so summons trigger local micro kiting (retreat for 18s) without corrupting strategic army-value accounting.
  2. 3-State Expansion Pad Belief (Confirmed-Owned | Confirmed-Empty(t_seen) | Unscouted):
    • Track exact last-cleared-frame for every expansion pad on the map using our unit sight radii!
    • Eliminate the dark-matter greed inversion in mirror-mind-predictor.rkt: unaccounted-minerals under unscouted fog must increase uncertainty + scouting priority + defensive army floor ("unknown threat in fog"), NEVER 'punish-And-Deny-Greedy-Expand! Only trigger 'punish-And-Deny-Greedy-Expand when we positively observe an early 3rd/4th TownHall with low enemy production/army.
  3. Production- & Income-Coupled Enemy Army Lower Bound ($\hat{A}_{\text{enemy}}$):
    • Estimate $\hat{A}_{\text{enemy}} = \max\!\big(A_{\text{persisted-tags}},\; \hat{I}_{\text{opp}}(t) - C_{\text{observed-econ-tech}} - L_{\text{enemy-killed}}\big)$ bounded by observed production facilities (8 Gateways -> opponent is producing up to 16 supply per warp cycle!).

P1 — Couple Our Army Floor ($A_{\min}^*$) to $\hat{A}_{\text{enemy}}$ & Fix the 3 Macro/Escrow Deviations

  1. Belief-Coupled Army Floor (Fixes #5131381 & #5131383 0.04x Steamrolls):
    • Instead of peaceful? = (enemy-threat-supply <= 0) allowing 72 Drones on 1,400 army against 9,925 Stalker/Immortal, set target minimum standing army $A_{\min}^*(t) = \max\!\big(A_{\text{curve}}(W),\; 0.70 \cdot \hat{A}_{\text{enemy}}\big)$ once W >= 32 (for Zerg, banking larva/minerals or building Roach/Hydra/Lurker waves whenever our army falls below 70% of believed enemy army + production output!).
  2. Imminent-Only Expansion Escrow (>= 250M) + In-Progress TownHall Worker Cap Credit (Fixes #5131383, #5132950, #5132985):
    • Never reserve 400M at 85M! Only activate TownHall expansion mineral floor once minerals >= 250M (or builder is en route), so Probes/SCVs build 100% continuously through 17 -> 18 -> 19 -> 20 right up to Nexus/CC placement!
    • Count an in-progress (>= 40% built) natural TownHall (and morphing Orbital's completion queue) toward max-useful-workers so 1-base Terran/Protoss never stalls at 18–20 workers (sched=()) while the natural finishes.
  3. Flexible T3 Tech Authorization & Follow-Up Expand Coupling (Refined from Rigid 3-Base Gate — Fixes #5132983 3:45 Ghost Academy & #5132984 Templar Archive Without Locking Out T3 Tech Rushes):
    • Initial Phase-1 guardrail: Gating non-emergency T3 tech structures (ghost-academy, fusion-core, templar-archive, fleet-beacon, ultralisk-cavern, greater-spire, dark-shrine) behind active-bases >= 3, workers >= 46, and army-supply >= 24 stopped generic macro templates from self-starving early production, but is too rigid because it locks out legitimate 1- or 2-base T3 tech-rush / timing builds (fast BC, DT drop, 2-base Storm/Archon, fast Skytoss) that deliberately tech first and expand immediately behind pressure.
    • Refined Wave-15 design (3-tier authorization):
      1. Committed Plan-Intent Exemption + Post-Tech Expand Coupling: When an active build plan or strategic counter explicitly commits to a T3 timing branch, bypass the generic 3-base wall (while requiring basic rush-survival safety) and automatically schedule the follow-up expansion behind the T3 structure as soon as it starts/completes.
      2. Continuous Opportunity-Cost / Throughput Gate (for default/unplanned macro templates): Replace the binary bases >= 3 cliff with economic throughput & production utilization — allowing T3 once either the standard 3-base foundation holds or a saturated 2-base gas-rich economy (workers >= 38, >= 3 geysers / gas surplus, core production unstarved, survival margin covered) can afford the T3 building + first payoff unit without idling workers or core production.
      3. Emergency Counter Override: Retain immediate bypass for enemy capital-air / mass-HT / cloak emergencies.

P2 — Symmetry-Agnostic Territory Control, Commitment-Risk Gating, Anti-Summon Kiting & Late-Game Siege Breakers

  1. Symmetry-Agnostic Ground-Path Voronoi Territory Partition & Dynamic Commitment-Risk Gate (Refined from Hard Mid-Map Rule):
    • Initial Phase-1 guardrail: Requiring our_army_value >= 1.12 * believed_enemy_army_value before crossing into unscouted fog prevented blind suicide pushes, but both Euclidean mid-map assumptions and a blanket mid-map wall are too rigid: SC2 maps vary across rotational 180°, horizontal/vertical mirror, diagonal reflection, and asymmetric/pocket layouts, and sound play frequently crosses mid-map without global army superiority.
    • Refined Wave-15 design:
      1. Map-Symmetry Inference + Dual Ground-BFS Voronoi Partition: At match start, infer map mirroring style (rotational-180, mirror-horizontal, mirror-vertical, mirror-diagonal, asymmetric) from start locations, playable bounds, and expansion pads, and precompute our-ground-bfs and enemy-ground-bfs (via existing compute-ground-bfs-distances) to obtain an O(1) ground-path territory margin m_ground(p) = d_ground_enemy(p) - d_ground_ours(p) (with air distance tracked separately for flying/harass units) dynamically anchored to live/believed bases.
      2. Commitment-Risk Gating Instead of a Hard Mid-Map Cutoff: Partition engagements into Own Territory (always defend), Contested Frontline Band (contestable for map control/vision/chokes/creep clearing), and Deep Enemy Territory (m_ground < -8), where ONLY slow, non-retreatable main-army commitment into unscouted lethal fog is gated on a continuous threshold (speed/escape margin + reinforcement distance vs. reachable believed enemy army). Explicitly exempt mobile/retreatable harass squads (Reapers, Hellions, Banshees, Medivac drops, Oracles, Phoenix, Warp Prisms, Adepts, Blink Stalkers, Mutalisks, Ling runbys), upgrade power-spike timings, exposed expansion denials, and behind-in-eco desperation pushes.
  2. Free-Summon Lifespan Disengage + Cooldown Counter-Push (#5132988 vs Swarm Hosts):
    • When >= 4 enemy locust / broodling / auto-turret units are active and no Swarm Host / Raven / Brood Lord body is in weapon range, kite back off creep until the 18s Locust timer expires, then immediately advance/Warp-Prism-hunt the 0-DPS Swarm Hosts during their 43s cooldown!
  3. Maxed-Out (>= 130 Supply / >= 4 Bases) Zerg/Protoss/Terran T3 Siege-Breaker Transition (#5131434, #5132986, #5131612):
    • Hard-cap Zergling count (<= 24 past 66 Drones unless enemy is pure Immortal/Tank in open field) and force Lurker Den + Seismic Spines -> Lurkers and Greater Spire -> Brood Lords + Viper (and Tempest/Colossus/Siege Tank + Liberator for P/T) whenever enemy has >= 2 Bunkers / Siege Tanks / Planetary Fortresses / >= 6 Spine-Photon static defense!

P3 — Early Cannon-Rush Pull Arithmetic (#5132982, #5132989) & Frame-0 Random Neutral Opening

  • Against an enemy Probe + Pylon/Cannon in/near Zerg main/natural (Creepy_strategies), assign 1 Drone trailing each Probe + 4 Drones focus-firing the lowest-progress Cannon/Pylon (never canceling Pool, rushing Spine + Ravagers) instead of switching into CrisisZealotRush at 16 Drones with 0 kills.
  • Against Random opponents (who, sc2-bot-next-random), use a safe vs-Random neutral wall/scout opening until the 14/16 worker scout confirms actual race, instead of guessing PvP on frame 0.
