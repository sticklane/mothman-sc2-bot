Pro-Replay Macro Analysis & Trajectory-First Prioritization Engine Design #

Key Takeaway

Executive Takeaway: Across 282 LotV 1v1 pro replays (564 player perspectives), 6,478 dual-perspective checkpoints, and 1,208 full-runtime spatial snapshots, our current engine diverges sharply from pro play because worker production and expansion are treated as conditional privileges that must pass ~14 veto gates, rather than as the default trajectory from which we deviate only for existential survival, bounded eco-disruption, or a planned timing/all-in window.

1.3 Code-Level Root Causes of Those Divergences #

  1. Hair-Trigger under-base-attack? Collapses Worker Utility to 2.8:
    • In bot-runtime.rkt, under-base-attack? flips #t whenever any enemy combat unit is within 28.0 tiles of an owned base or live-enemy-threat-supply > max(4, live-army-supply) anywhere on the map.
    • In priority-macro-brain.rkt, need-worker-saturation? begins with (and (not under-attack?) ...). The instant 2 Stalkers or 6 Zerglings are visible, worker utility drops from 430.0 to 2.8 (priority-macro-brain.rkt) — letting the opponent "kill" 25–30 virtual workers without firing a shot.
  2. Zero Worker Mineral Reservation + Layer-2 >= 18 Worker Cutoff:
    • In priority-macro-brain.rkt, workers set blocking-allowed? = #f once workers >= 8, so every 100–150M structure/addon/unit drains the mineral pool before 50M workers are scheduled.
    • In bot-runtime.rkt (terran-scv-priority?), bot-runtime.rkt (protoss-probe-priority?), and bot-runtime.rkt (zerg-drone-priority?), once total-workers >= 18, Layer 2 forces workers behind cumulative scheduled combat/tech reserves and l2-worker-army-curve-satisfied? (bot-runtime.rkt) — directly causing the exact 18-SCV plateau seen in 5 of 10 post-deploy Terran games (#5130626, #5131128, #5131270, #5131272, #5131274).
  3. Unbranched Build Orders & Broken Step Ordering:
    • build-orders.rkt maps each matchup to one static linear list with zero branching on scouted pool/hatch/gas timings:
      • pvz-1-gate-fe-stargate-oracle-archon.rkt unconditionally puts 19-Nexus before cybernetics-core even against a 12-pool, and mixes incompatible stargate, templar-archive, and robotics-bay unit benchmarks.
      • tvz-reaper-fe-1-1-1-hellion-banshee.rkt puts barracks-techlab (step 6) ahead of factory (step 7) and barracks-reactor (step 15), causing Terran to open TechLab + Concussive Shells (0 Hellions) into 3:50 Speedling floods.
      • tvp-reaper-fe-3-rax-stim-medivac.rkt takes a 2nd Refinery at 0:53 (22 supply) before natural saturation, starving SCV/Barracks minerals while floating 370–400G.
      • zvt-16-hatch-macro-roach-ravager.rkt and zvp-16-hatch-3-base-roach-hydra.rkt terminate their build-order steps at 68 supply (grooved-spines) with no lurker-den, infestation-pit, hive, spire, or greater-spire steps.

3. Proposed Architecture: Trajectory-First Macro Prioritization Engine #

To keep the engine clean, declarative, and completely free of tangled conditionals, we separate Strategy & Trajectory -> Regime Selection -> Constrained Marginal-Value Allocation -> Stateless Spatial Execution:

flowchart TD
    S["1. Scout & Opponent State (Pool/Hatch/Gas Timings, Effective Worker/Tech/Upgrade Diff)"] --> T["2. Branching Build-Order Tree (Selects Active Branch & Trajectory Targets W*, B*, G*, M*)"]
    T --> R["3. Explicit 4-State Macro Regime Automaton"]
    S --> R
    R -->|"HAPPY_PATH_MACRO (Default)"| A["4. Resource-Constrained Marginal-Value Allocator (Minerals, Gas, Supply, Facility Slots / Larva)"]
    R -->|"SURVIVAL_DEFENSE (Arrival Deficit > 0)"| A
    R -->|"ECO_DISRUPTION_INVESTMENT (Capped Harass Unit)"| A
    R -->|"TIMING_OR_ALLIN_WINDOW (Planned Cut w/ Abort)"| A
    A -->|"Exact Feasible AllocationPlan"| L2["5. Stateless Layer-2 Executor (Placement + Order-Ledger Only, 0 Policy Vetoes)"]

3.1 Component 1: Branching Pro Build-Order Graphs (lib/build-orders/) #

Replace each single static list with a small declarative Build-Order Graph (2–4 branches per matchup) keyed on observable scouting predicates:

  • Scouted Opponent Predicates (computed once per step in mirror-mind / econ-ledger):
    • enemy-pool-started-est-sec (inferred from building progress if scouted building, 12-pool if ready < 75s or Lings at our base < 125s, pool-first if < 95s),
    • enemy-natural-started-est-sec (#f if scouted natural pad empty after 65s vs Zerg or 105s vs P/T),
    • enemy-gas-count-and-mined (gasless vs 1-gas speed vs 2-gas roach/ravager/stargate/factory all-in).
  • Example PvZ Graph:
    • Root (0:00–1:15): 14 Pylon -> 16 Gate (Gate-scout!) -> 17 Gas.
    • Branch A (enemy_pool_est < 48s or no_zerg_nat_by_68s) -> Anti-Early-Pool Core-First: 18 CyberCore -> 19 Zealot (Chrono) -> 20 Pylon (complete wall) -> ShieldBattery + Adept/Stalker -> Nexus once natural choke is walled & held -> converge back to 3-Base Macro.
    • Branch B (Standard Hatch-First) -> 20 Nexus -> 20 CyberCore -> 21 2nd Gas -> 22 Pylon -> coherent Stargate Oracle -> Twilight/Robo Charge-Immortal-Archon or Blink-Robo-Colossus (never mixing incompatible T3 benchmarks in one branch!).
  • Example TvZ Graph:
    • Fix step ordering: 14 Depot -> 16 Rax -> 16 Gas -> 19 Reaper + Orbital -> (High-Ground CC if 12-pool, else Low-Ground CC @ 20) -> BarracksReactor + Factory -> swap Factory onto Reactor for 4–6 Hellions (shutting down 3:50 Speedling floods!) -> Starport -> 3rd CC -> 2x Rax + Stim + Combat Shield + EBay.

3.2 Component 2: Explicit 4-State Macro Regime Automaton #

Instead of under-base-attack? shutting off workers at 28 tiles, compute one explicit regime per step:

  1. HAPPY_PATH_MACRO (Default):
    • Active whenever SurvivalDeficit <= 0 and the build branch is not inside an explicit TimingCut / AllInCut window.
    • TownHall Worker Lease (T/P): Reserve $50 \times \text{OpenTownHalls}$ minerals ahead of non-emergency structures and extra units whenever $W < W^*(t)$.
    • Zerg Drone-Wave Default: Allocate 100% of larvae to Drones whenever $W < W^*(t)$ and $A_{\text{ours}} \ge A_{\text{safety}}(\hat{A}_{\text{opp}})$.
  2. SURVIVAL_DEFENSE (Opponent Not Macroing / Imminent Lethal Push):
    • Triggered only when: $$\Delta_{\text{survive}} = \hat{A}_{\text{advancing}}(\tau_{\text{arrive}}) - \big(A_{\text{ours}} + D_{\text{wall/static}} + P_{\text{open}}(\tau_{\text{arrive}})\big) > 0$$ where $\hat{A}_{\text{advancing}}$ requires actual enemy combat force within 20 tiles of an owned base OR confirmed opponent worker/base deficit (1-base/2-base all-in).
    • Crucial T/P Rule: Even during SURVIVAL_DEFENSE, only pause a 50M SCV/Probe if Minerals - 50 < OpenCombatFacilityMineralNeed (i.e. making the worker would literally leave an open Barracks/Gateway/Robo idle this step!).
    • Crucial Zerg Rule: Flip 100% of larvae to Pure Army Wave (zergling/baneling/roach/hydralisk) until $\Delta_{\text{survive}} \le 0$, then snap straight back to Pure Drone Wave.
  3. ECO_DISRUPTION_INVESTMENT (Bounded Harass Tool):
    • Grants temporary priority to a strictly count-capped harass unit from the active branch (1 Reaper, 4 Hellions, 1–2 Oracles, 2 Adepts, 1 Mine-Medivac drop) without suppressing worker leases.
  4. TIMING_OR_ALLIN_WINDOW (Planned Worker Cut + Auto-Transition):
    • Holds $W$ at the branch's timing cap ($W^_{\text{cut}}$, e.g. 41 or 46 in 2-base TvP, 32 in 2-base Roach/Ravager) to surge production slots for the push, with an explicit Hold/Abort Condition: if the push fails to kill a base/workers within $\Delta t_{\text{window}}$ or trades into defender advantage, abort to HAPPY_PATH_MACRO ($W^ \to 66$, start next TownHall).

3.3 Component 3: Unified Economic Allocator (Expansion, Gas Tax & Tech Parity) #

Every non-emergency decision follows four clean, composable economic laws rather than hardcoded booleans:

  1. Mineral-Driven Base Expansion (B*):
    • Start next TownHall when mineral_harvesters >= 14 * active_bases (pre-building ~25s before full 16/base mineral saturation so workers transfer seamlessly on completion), pending_townhalls == 0, and regime $\neq \text{ALLIN\_WINDOW}$.
  2. Demand-Driven Geyser Controller (G*) & Dynamic Gas Pull:
    • Define NetGas30s = GasBank + 30s * GasIncomeRate - 30s * PlannedDAGAndUnitGasDemand.
    • Build next Geyser iff:
      1. Opening step explicitly calls for Gas #1/#2, OR
      2. mineral_harvesters >= 15 * min(2, active_bases) (>= 30 mineral workers before Gas #3/#4, >= 26 mineral workers + 3 bases before Zerg Gas #2) AND NetGas30s < 50G AND GasBank < 250G.
    • Gas-to-Mineral Worker Rebalance: Whenever GasBank > 300G and Minerals < 150M while $W < W^*$, pull 3 harvesters off gas onto minerals until GasBank < 175G.
  3. Monotonic Tech & Upgrade Parity Escalator (Fixing Zerg T1 Lock & Premature T3):
    • Score next unlocked DAG tech/upgrade node (Lair -> HydraDen/BaneNest -> LurkerDen/Hive/Spire, Twilight/Robo -> RoboBay/TemplarArchive, Factory -> Starport -> EBay/Armory) as a smooth function of:
      • Economic Readiness: $\max(0, W - W_{\text{tier\_min}})$ (Lair at W >= 32, Hive/RoboBay/GhostAcademy at W >= 44–50),
      • Parity Pressure: boosts utility when opponent tech/upgrade tier leads ours ($\hat{\text{Tier}}_{\text{opp}} > \text{Tier}_{\text{ours}}$ or $\hat{\text{Upg}}_{\text{opp}} > \text{Upg}_{\text{ours}}$),
      • Gas Sink Relief: scales upward smoothly whenever GasBank >= 200G.
  4. Stateless Layer-2 Execution (Single Source of Policy Truth):
    • Strip policy vetoes out of bot-runtime.rkt (terran-scv-priority?, protoss-probe-priority?, zerg-drone-priority?, l2-worker-army-curve-satisfied?, (>= total-workers 18) worker reserve block): Phase 8 emits an exact feasible AllocationPlan per facility slot, and Layer 2 executes placement + order-ledger 1-for-1.
