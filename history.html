How We Test and Ship Changes

Every update goes through the same seven-step check before reaching the live ladder. After each pass, a script also checks our build and test logs to see where time was wasted or which rules kept getting edited back and forth.

1. Live & Practice Games
Step 1 · Replays
What happens here

Twelve Mothman bots play continuously on AI Arena and across local practice batches. Every replay is parsed into minute-by-minute worker counts, army values, expansion timings, and fault flags.

Why it matters

Win rate bounces around over ten-game samples. Recording economy curves and fault flags catches silent bugs, such as production stalling mid-game, even in matches Mothman wins.

Notable Bugs Found and Fixed

Six recurring bugs caught in replays and combat drills, what caused them in the code, and what changed after fixing them.

Geometry & Placement · Fixed in Loop 37

Losing More Often from Top-Half Spawns

Symptom: Bots won 16 percentage points less often when spawning north-east on Ley Lines (21.7% vs. 37.8%) and trailed by nearly 5 points across all northern spawns.

Root cause: Building placement and early scout offsets assumed the enemy sat roughly up and to the right, so top-half spawns walled off their own second base.

Fix & result: Replaced compass-direction offsets with actual ground walking distances. Across 1,137 local matches since Loop 37, north and south win rates leveled out (29.0% vs. 29.6%), and recent builds split within 1 percentage point across north and south spawns.

Enemy Tech Inference · Fixed in Loop 43

Dead Zerg Buildings Triggering Anti-Air Panic

Symptom: Against pure ground Zerg (Roaches and Hydralisks), Terran suddenly built 4 Starports and 13 Vikings mid-game.

Root cause: Destroying any Zerg building spawns short-lived Broodlings. Our tech-prerequisite table mapped Broodlings to Brood Lords and a Greater Spire, which tricked Terran into panicking against non-existent air units.

Fix & result: Ignored Broodlings and Sentry hallucinations in tech inference. Unnecessary Viking builds dropped to zero and lifted Terran above a 1.0× overall trade ratio.

Unit Control · Fixed in Loops 43–46

Dark Templars Attacking Before Clearing Detectors

Symptom: In drill p15, Dark Templars blinked straight onto a Terran Planetary Fortress while both Missile Turrets were still standing and died to splash damage. Even when they killed the turrets, surviving Dark Templars merged into visible Archons or got recalled home by their own Nexus.

Fix & result: Dark Templars now split into two groups to destroy nearby detectors first, stay cloaked instead of merging into Archons or taking Mass Recall when no enemy detection remains, and sweep unexplored fog tiles. Drill p15 flipped from a 0.33× loss to a 1.58× win with 14 of 27 Dark Templars surviving.

Spending & Production · Fixed in Loop 48

Conflicting Barracks Limits Freezing Terran Production

Symptom: In Terran mirrors and three benchmark games, Terran stopped building units for several seconds at a time while sitting on unspent minerals.

Root cause: The spending queue scheduled a 5th or 7th Barracks on three bases, while the build executor enforced an older cap of two Barracks per Command Center and quietly dropped the order every frame.

Fix & result: Matched the executor's facility cap (up to 8) to the spending queue and added a unit test verifying that both modules agree on every building limit.

Testing Workflow · Shipped in Loop 48

Running a 2-Minute Check Before the 30-Minute Benchmark

Symptom: Six of seven held builds lost their 2-minute head-to-head match against the live bot, yet our test script ran the 31-minute, 84-game benchmark first and wasted 186 minutes of simulation.

Fix & result: Moved the 2-minute head-to-head gate and targeted combat drills ahead of the full 84-game benchmark, and added a warning whenever the same rule gets tweaked repeatedly across consecutive runs.

StarCraft II 5.0.14 Protocol & Regression Hygiene · Loops 50–57

Stale Ability IDs and Stacked Macro Locks

Symptom: Several high-tech abilities silently failed on patch 5.0.14 (Baneling morph 80 vs. 4121, Raven Auto-Turret 349 vs. 1764, Overseer Oversight 3745 vs. 3743, Microbial Shroud 2545 vs. 4111), and stacking macro escrow locks across Loops 52–55 dragged win rates down despite head-to-head gains.

Fix & result: Updated all 5.0.14 ability IDs with dynamic upgrade checks, then rebased cleanly onto Build 51 keeping only verified zero-downside fixes (Supply Depot lowering, Overseer cap, Stalker-before-Adept priority, and Spore cancel guard) for Release #10.

Economy stalls addressed in Loops 49–51

Across Loops 42–48 and earlier ladder builds, most macro losses traced to seven recurring economy stalls. Loops 49 through 51 shipped four general fallback checks to unstick blocked builds, rebalance mineral and gas workers, build supply ahead of production spikes, and resume halted structures. Those checks cut high-gas/low-mineral imbalances by 58% (12 to 5), zero-income stalls by 43% (7 to 4), and escrow lockouts by 41% (17 to 10).
