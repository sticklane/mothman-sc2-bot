1. Executive Summary

Key Takeaway

Production Stability is 100% Clean: Across all 80/80 live AI Arena matches (3 builds, 7 ladder maps, 23 external bots + 6 internal self-play matches), Mothman recorded 0 crashes, 0 stderr runtime errors, and 0 step spikes >= 500ms (max single step across all 80 matches: 380.55ms). The only 2 crash results in the dataset (#5137816, #5137815) were 0.2s opponent crashes by sc2-bot-next-random (Mothman wins).

Watch Item

Three Headline Live-Prod Bottlenecks Dominate Losses (74 external matches = 41W-29L-4T, 55.4% WR / 60.8% NL):

  1. TvP is 1W-8L-0T (11.1% WR), PvT is 3W-5L-1T (33.3% WR), and PvP is 3W-3L-2T (37.5% WR) — these 3 matchups account for 19/33 (57.6%) of all external losses+ties, while the other 6 matchups (TvZ 7-0, ZvZ 6-1, TvT 6-3, PvZ 5-3, ZvT 6-4, ZvP 4-2-1) combine for 34W-13L-1T (70.8% WR / 72.9% NL).
  2. Protoss Gateway-Robo Deathballs + Heavy Siege-Tank Lines (DripFeed + Siege0DPS) cause 17/33 (51.5%) of all external losses+ties (PrismBot 0-4, PrismForge 0-2-2, CutterSlayer 0-3-1, CosmicSiege 1-2, GhostProtocol 2-2) — often in games where Mothman is ahead on workers at m06.
  3. Severe North-vs-South Spawn-Corner Bias (-27.3pp overall, -43.6pp on Protoss): External prod WR is 69.4% (25W-10L-1T) from South (SW/SE) vs 42.1% (16W-19L-3T) from North (NW/NE) — with Protoss collapsing from 66.7% (8-4-0) South to 23.1% (3-7-3) North, corroborating @loop-architect's #833 1,595-game local SQLite finding (SW 33.3% vs NW 25.0%).
