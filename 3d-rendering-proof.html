2. Why Our First Probe Failed & How We Unlocked It #

  1. Missing OpenGL Shader Cache in aiarena Docker Image: Disassembling SC2_x64's Failed to load shaders path revealed it requires Versions/Shaders41359/OpenGL2/baselineCache.bin (603 MB uncompressed). docker.io/aiarena/arenaclient-combined:v0.8.0 stripped that folder to shrink their headless container.
    • Fix (already cached locally): We byte-range fetched just that 52.4 MB entry from Blizzard's official SC2.4.10.zip (no 4.1 GB download needed!) and extracted it to sc2-local-arena/shaders/StarCraftII/Versions/Shaders41359/OpenGL2/baselineCache.bin.
  2. Strix Halo GPU Driver Version: The container's Debian 12 Mesa (22.3.6) predates AMD Strix Halo (gfx1151), so it fell back to software llvmpipe. Launching SC2_x64 against your host's Mesa 25.0.7 (radeonsi) via -eglpath /usr/lib/x86_64-linux-gnu/libEGL.so.1 unlocks full hardware OpenGL 4.6 acceleration.
  3. Graphics Quality: Supplying a clean Variables.txt (GraphicsOptionOverallQualityVer7[5]=3) eliminates low-shader footprint artifacts and turns on crisp lighting, cast shadows, and particle effects.
