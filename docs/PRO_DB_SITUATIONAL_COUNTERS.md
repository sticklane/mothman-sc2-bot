## 1. Executive Summary & Mothman Baseline Root Causes

- **Pro Corpus Coverage**: `1698` replays scanned (`278` valid 1v1 LotV games, `556` player trajectories; `1649` valid decoded replays / `9907` horizon checkpoints across `15s..130s`; `298` LotV replays / `596` perspectives in `data/pro_build_orders_summary.json`).
- **AIArena Baseline Failure Pattern (`replays/aiarena_matches/forensic_summary.json`)**: Across `26` matches (`2W-22L-2T`, `7.7%` win rate), **`20/26` matches flagged `COUNTER_UNIT_MISMATCH`** and **`13/26` matches flagged `DELAYED_NATURAL_EXPANSION` (>180s)** (`197.9s-263.5s` vs pro medians `48.7s-136.6s`).
- **Runtime & Predictor Defects Corrected**:
  1. `lib/mirror-mind-predictor.rkt` (baseline `L3422`, `archetype-signature-unit`) previously returned `'banshee` as Terran's counter to `'sky-toss`/`'air-fleet` (**Banshees cannot shoot air**); fixed to return phase/tech/air-ratio-aware `'viking-fighter` / `'thor` / `'marine`.
  2. Protoss ground armies lacking Stargate/air entities were misclassified as `'sky-toss` because Terran Starport units (`viking`, `banshee`, `battlecruiser`, `liberator`, `raven`) were grouped under `'sky-toss` in `unit-to-archetype`.
  3. Uncalibrated `win-prob` / `snowball-adv` allowed raw structure counts and unspent banks to inflate win probability to `0.73-1.00` while losing on workers, combat army supply, and trade ratio.
  4. `lib/situational-counter-planner.rkt` lacked phase-aware gas starvation gates (`worker < 40` / unfinished natural), `<= 1.25x` anti-air specialist supply caps (`corruptor`/`viking-fighter`), and late-game (`loop > 13440`) fragile T1 penalties (`zergling`, un-upgraded `marine`, un-charged `zealot`) vs splash.
